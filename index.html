Getting There

Fly to Keflavík

Direct flights to Keflavík International Airport (KEF). Collect your 4WD SUV on arrival — a 4WD SUV is recommended for the rugged South Coast roads. From the airport the adventure begins immediately.

✈
Keflavík Airport (KEF)
Direct flights from London. Journey time approx. 3 hours. Keflavík is 50km from Reykjavík and 20 minutes from the Blue Lagoon — perfect for heading straight there on arrival.
DIRECT FLIGHT
🚗
4WD SUV Rental
Pick up your vehicle at KEF. A capable 4WD SUV is essential for the South Coast — F-roads require high clearance. Book in advance for June, as demand is high.
PICK UP AT KEF4WD REQUIRED
🌟 June in Iceland — Midnight Sun
In late June you’ll have nearly 24 hours of daylight. The sun barely sets, creating spectacular golden light at “midnight”. This lets you drive late, see waterfalls in extraordinary light, and explore without rushing. Pack an eye mask for sleeping.
Day 1 · Sunday 21 June

The Geothermal Reset

Land at KEF, collect your 4WD, and drive 20 minutes directly to the Blue Lagoon. The perfect way to shake off the flight and arrive in Iceland feeling completely renewed.

Arrival
KEF

Collect your 4WD SUV at the airport and drive directly south for 20 minutes. No hotel check-in, no faff — straight to the lagoon.

Afternoon
♨
Blue Lagoon — Retreat Spa
Soak in the world-famous geothermal waters surrounded by black lava fields. The milky-blue silica-rich water sits at a perfect 38–40°C. Book the Retreat experience for exclusive access away from the main crowds, with in-water bar service and silica mud masks.
GEOTHERMAL SPABOOK IN ADVANCE20 MINS FROM KEF

~1h 45m

After the lagoon, drive 125km east along the Ring Road to Hella. The landscape shifts from lunar lava fields to vivid green farmland.

Stay
🏠
Landhótel, Hella
Smart, comfortable hotel in Hella with excellent facilities and a warm Icelandic welcome. A great South Coast base with everything you need after the first big day.
4 STARHELLA
Day 2 · Monday 22 June

The Golden Circle

A 180km round trip from Hella taking in Iceland’s three most iconic natural wonders. The geology here is staggering — tectonic plates, erupting geysers, and a thundering glacial waterfall.

Morning
🌏
þingvellir National Park
Walk the rift valley where the North American and Eurasian tectonic plates are visibly pulling apart. Iceland’s most historically and geologically significant site. A UNESCO World Heritage Site.
UNESCO SITETECTONIC PLATES
⚔ Game of Thrones: This rift valley appeared as the rocky path to the Bloody Gate in Season 4 — where Arya and the Hound were turned away.
💦
Strokkur Geysir
The famous Strokkur geyser erupts every 5–10 minutes, sending a column of boiling water 20–40 metres into the sky. Unmissable, and spectacular to photograph in the June light.
ERUPTS EVERY 10 MINS
Midday
🌊
Gullfoss Waterfall
The “Golden Falls” — a massive two-tiered waterfall plunging into a dramatic canyon. In June the flow is at its peak from glacial melt. Walk to the viewing platforms and feel the mist.
GLACIAL WATERFALLJUNE PEAK FLOW
Evening

Return to Hella for dinner. Hotel Rangá has a celebrated restaurant with Icelandic lamb and Arctic char — reserve a table for a relaxed evening ahead of tomorrow’s big day.

Day 3 · Wednesday 23 June 2027 · THE BIRTHDAY
🎉 Jack turns 30 🎉
Celebrated on a glacier, under the midnight sun, in the most dramatic landscape on earth

Snowmobiling on a Volcano

Drive 35 minutes to Brú Base Camp for snowmobiling on the Eyjafjallajökull volcano glacier — the very volcano whose 2010 eruption shut down European airspace. A Super Jeep takes you from base camp up to the snowline for a 1-hour snowmobile ride with 360° views of the South Coast and the Westman Islands.

Morning
Birthday
❄
Snowmobiling on Eyjafjallajökull
Meeting point: Brú Base Camp — approx. 35 minutes from Hella. A Super Jeep drives you up to the glacier summit, where you ride snowmobiles across vast white ice with the entire South Coast spread below you. In June the glacier is thick and the visibility extraordinary.
BIRTHDAY ADVENTURE35 MINS FROM HELLA1 HR SNOWMOBILE RIDE
Afternoon
💧
Seljalandsfoss — Walk Behind the Falls
A short drive from the glacier base. Seljalandsfoss is one of Iceland’s most spectacular waterfalls — and uniquely, you can walk behind the curtain of water into the cave behind it. In the eternal June daylight the light through the falling water is extraordinary.
WALK BEHIND THE FALLSMIDNIGHT SUN LIGHT
Birthday
Dinner
🍽
Hafið Bláa — The Blue Harbour
Celebratory birthday feast at this beloved riverside and ocean-view restaurant near Selfoss. Famous for fresh Icelandic fish, lamb, and a warm atmosphere. Book well in advance for a window table.
BIRTHDAY FEASTRIVER & OCEAN VIEWSBOOK IN ADVANCE
Day 4 · Thursday 24 June

Glaciers & Black Sands

A spectacular day on the ice at Sólheimajökull glacier — with an activity split so Jack and Daisy can take on the technical climb while Julian and Lianne enjoy a more relaxed glacier walk on the same ice tongue.

11:00am
Glacier
⚡ Jack & Daisy
Blue Ice Hike & Climbing
4.5 hours — Technical ice climb and high-glacier trek. Crampons, ice axes, the full kit. Scrambling up seracs and exploring deep blue crevasses. The full Sólheimajökull experience for the adventurous.
4.5 HRSTECHNICAL CLIMB
🧖 Julian & Lianne
Glacier Experience Walk
3 hours — Easy, informative, and flat walk on the ice tongue with a knowledgeable guide. See crevasses, moulins, and glacial formations close-up without the technical climbing element.
3 HRSEASY ACCESS
🕐 Both groups meet at the base van at 11:00am — Arctic Adventures · Sólheimajökull glacier
⚔ Game of Thrones: Sólheimajökull is an outlet of Mýrdalsjökull — the glacier icescapes used for Beyond the Wall scenes, the Fist of the First Men, and the White Walker marches in Seasons 2–4.
Afternoon
💧
Kvernufoss — Hidden Waterfall
A short hike from the road to a stunning hidden waterfall that most visitors miss. You can walk behind this one too. Far less crowded than Skógafoss next door.
HIDDEN GEM
🌊
Skógafoss
One of Iceland’s most powerful and photogenic waterfalls — a 60-metre curtain of water. Climb the 527 steps to the top for panoramic South Coast views.
60M WATERFALLCLIFF TOP VIEWS

🏠
Hotel Vík í Mýrdal

Day 5 · Friday 25 June

Kingdom of Puffins
& Return to Reykjavík

A short morning drive to the dramatic Dyrhólaey cliffs to watch puffins nesting, then the Reynisfjara black sand beach, before the long drive back to the capital.

Morning
15 mins
🐦
Dyrhólaey Cliffs & Puffins
Drive to the Dyrhólaey promontory and use the 4WD to reach the upper lighthouse. In June the Atlantic puffins are nesting on the clifftops — you can get remarkably close. The arched rock formation over the sea and views to Reynisfjara beach are stunning.
PUFFIN NESTING SEASON4WD TO LIGHTHOUSE
Mid-
morning
⬛
Reynisfjara Black Sand Beach
Walk the dramatic black volcanic sand beach with its towering basalt column formations. The waves here are powerful — never turn your back on the sea. One of Iceland’s most striking and otherworldly landscapes.
BLACK VOLCANIC SANDRESPECT THE WAVESBASALT COLUMNS
⚔ Game of Thrones: Used as the coastal landscape near Eastwatch-by-the-Sea in Season 7 — where Jon Snow’s party marched north beyond the Wall.
Drive
~2h 30m

Drive 190km west along the Ring Road back to Reykjavík. The route is one of the most scenic in Iceland — don’t rush it.

Stay
🏢
þingholt by Center Hotels
Boutique design hotel in central Reykjavík. Walking distance from everything — restaurants, the harbour, Hallgrímskirkja church, and the city’s legendary bars.

Day 6 · Saturday 26 June

Whale Watching & Grand Finale

The final full day. A morning on the water searching for humpbacks and minke whales, followed by the celebratory tasting menu dinner of the trip.

Morning
🐋
Whale Watching — Elding, Old Harbour
Board a Classic Big Boat from Reykjavík’s Old Harbour. June is one of the best months for whale sightings — humpbacks and minke whales are regularly seen in the rich feeding waters of Faxáflói Bay. Knowledgeable marine biologist guides on board.
HUMPBACK & MINKEJUNE PEAK SEASONOLD HARBOUR
Afternoon
Free

Explore Reykjavík — the world’s most northerly capital. Walk up to Hallgrímskirkja for city views, browse the shops on Laugavegur, or simply relax at a café.

Grand
Finale
🍽
Grillmarkadurinn — The Grillmarket
Reykjavík’s most celebrated restaurant. The grand finale tasting menu features exceptional Icelandic produce — langoustines, lamb, Arctic char, and local cheeses — paired with Icelandic spirits. One of the finest dining experiences in the North Atlantic.
GRAND FINALE DINNERTASTING MENUBOOK IN ADVANCE

Brað & Co
